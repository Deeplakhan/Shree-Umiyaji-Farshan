    About us

    Made for the neighbourhood.

    A farsan shop in Ankleshwar GIDC, known by the name Chandukaka Gathiyawala.

    Farsan is the Gujarati word for savoury snacks.

    It covers the crisp, salty and spiced things people reach for with tea, on a festival morning, or on the way home. Gathiya, fafda and sev sit at the centre of that tradition.

    Shree Umiyaji Farshan keeps its focus there, with a counter where you can dine in, collect at the kerb, or order delivery. Choose a suitable quantity and confirm your order with us directly.

    You will find us at Maurya Residency, near Jaldhara Chowkdi in Ankleshwar GIDC.
